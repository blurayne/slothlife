A SLOTH'S LIFE
A windy-tree adventure
HOW TO PLAY
  • Tap a branch to swing the sloth there.
  • Tap an apple (+10 pts) to eat it.
  • Tap a leaf (+1 pt) for a snack.
  • Survive a full in-game month for +50 pts.
  • Swipe the grass to scroll the scene left or right.
  • The hunger bar drains constantly — keep eating!
  • When the sloth is sleeping, less energy is consumed.
  • Press space or tap the clock to pause.
  • Falls and starvation cost a life. Three lives total.
  • Eat every apple to win. Each remaining life adds +250 pts.

TIP — A full game runs ~45 min (30 in-game months ≈ 2½ years across 3 calendar years). To end early, tap and hold the sloth for 3 seconds to kill it — it turns black and dies. Three deaths end the game.
